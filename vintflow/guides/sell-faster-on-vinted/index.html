How to sell clothes faster on Vinted

Improve photo quality, descriptions, pricing and buyer communication without automated marketplace actions.

Try free pricing toolOwner access
Public guides and pricing tools are open. The app is temporarily owner-only.

Make the first photo useful

Use a clean, evenly lit photo of the actual item. Make sure the full silhouette is visible. Include close-ups of the label, measurements where relevant, and flaws. Vinted's uploading guide explains how to describe and photograph an item honestly.

Set a competitive price

Compare several similar garments instead of anchoring on the highest listing. If a quick sale matters, select a price toward the lower end of suitable comparisons, but check purchase cost and expenses first. No discount can guarantee that an item will sell.

Reduce uncertainty for buyers

List the tagged size and actual measurements, especially for vintage garments. Describe defects clearly. Reply to reasonable questions promptly and propose a counteroffer only within your chosen profit target.

Review listings that are not moving

If interest is low, check whether the photos, title, category and price reflect the correct item. Record when you listed it and review performance after a reasonable period. Don't spam or use unauthorised bots. VintFlow's Inventory and Copilot tools help plan and review your own listings.

VintFlow AI offers editable suggestions, not certified valuations or guaranteed sales. Always review your item and publish it yourself.
