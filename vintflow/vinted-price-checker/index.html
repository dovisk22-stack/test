What is a Vinted price checker?

A resale price checker helps you decide what to ask for a garment by comparing similar listings. VintFlow AI researches indexed UK marketplace listings from the description detected in your photos, and displays source links when available. It is an independent tool, not Vinted's official pricing service.

How to compare the right items

Match the brand, garment, model or style, size and condition. A new item with tags is not equivalent to one with stains or repairs. Check whether the listed price excludes delivery and buyer charges, and prefer comparable completed transactions where you have access to them.

Three ways to price a listing

Quick Sale

A lower asking price for sellers who prioritise turnover.

Recommended

A balanced starting price supported by available information.

Higher Return

A higher ask with room to negotiate, if demand supports it.

These are selling strategies, not predictions of when an item will sell.

How reliable is the result?

Look at the evidence label and matched listings rather than relying on a number alone. When readable prices or sufficiently similar items are unavailable, guidance can be approximate or no estimate may be shown. Asking prices are not confirmed sales. Check eBay's Product Research and Vinted's pricing guidance before committing.

VintFlow AI offers editable suggestions, not certified valuations or guaranteed sales. Always review your item and publish it yourself.
