Calculate your minimum selling price

Your minimum target price is purchase cost plus seller-paid expenses plus desired profit. If you paid £5 for an item, expect £1 in expenses and want £4 profit, your target floor is £10. This is a cost-based decision, not a market valuation.

Try the calculator

Enter your figures to calculate a target price. Example amounts are editable.

Figures are calculated on your device. The suggested quick-sale scenario is 15% below the comparison price, not a guarantee of demand or timing.

Compare the floor with the market

If comparable garments are listed for less than your cost-based floor, raising the price may not solve the problem. Consider a smaller profit target, a different sales channel, holding the item or declining an offer. Do not automatically accept a loss.

Choose a selling strategy

Use the photo price checker for market context and open Copilot in the workspace to check offer profit and prepare a personal counteroffer. Read our pricing strategy guide for practical trade-offs.

VintFlow AI offers editable suggestions, not certified valuations or guaranteed sales. Always review your item and publish it yourself.
