How to sell clothes on Vinted: a UK beginner checklist

Start with clean, accurately described items and a repeatable selling routine. A good listing helps buyers make informed decisions.

Scan an item

Before taking photos

Check that the item is allowed under Vinted’s current catalogue rules. Read the size and brand labels, inspect for stains or holes and clean the garment appropriately. Do not misrepresent authenticity or condition.

Prepare a useful listing

Use a descriptive title, clear photos and accurate condition. Mention any faults and relevant measurements. Price using similar items rather than relying only on what you originally paid.

Before sending the parcel

Follow the shipping instructions on the marketplace. Check that the item matches the order, use suitable packaging and retain the relevant proof of dispatch. Keep communication and payment within the marketplace’s approved process.
