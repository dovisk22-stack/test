What is VintFlow AI?

VintFlow AI is the independent web-based resale workspace at vintflow.co.uk. It is designed for people selling secondhand and vintage clothing, footwear, accessories and other eligible resale items in the UK, with photo-based draft creation, market price comparisons, stock and profit tracking, and seller-approved reply and negotiation tools. It is not a Chrome extension and is unrelated to similarly named browser extensions. Our official website is vintflow.co.uk and our support address is vintflowhelp@gmail.com.

Our approach to responsible AI

The scanner suggests details visible in your photos and may search public indexed marketplace listings. Results are editable. We distinguish comparison-based estimates from approximate guidance and encourage checking the actual garment, condition and source listings. We do not guarantee age, authenticity, price or sales.

Membership and privacy

The free public guides and price calculator remain open during maintenance. The private workspace is limited to existing authorised owners while we test registration, billing and scanning. Existing membership and cancellation arrangements are unchanged. Inventory remains in the current browser workspace; you can export a backup.

Independent, not affiliated

VintFlow AI is not owned by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Vinted, eBay or Depop. Their names identify marketplaces that sellers may use. The platform helps sellers prepare their own listings; it does not automatically post or message on those services.

Get in touch

For account, billing, inaccurate recognition or price feedback, email vintflowhelp@gmail.com. Include the issue and relevant non-sensitive screenshots; never send passwords or API keys.

VintFlow AI offers editable suggestions, not certified valuations or guaranteed sales. Always review your item and publish it yourself.
