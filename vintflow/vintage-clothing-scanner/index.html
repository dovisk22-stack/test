What does a vintage clothing scanner do?

A clothing scanner helps turn a picture of an unfamiliar garment into searchable information: the likely garment type, visible brand, colour and readable size. VintFlow AI prepares an editable draft and looks for comparable UK resale listings. A photo alone cannot prove a date, authenticity or fabric content.

Photograph these three things

  1. The complete garment in neutral light, with no filter or hanger obscuring the neckline.
  2. The brand label, care tag, size tag and any style or product code, with text in focus.
  3. Distinctive construction and any damage, including cuffs, closures, stains or repairs.

Use photos of the same item. Keep original photographs for the final marketplace listing.

From identification to a sale

Review the AI draft, correct the brand or size if necessary, and choose the condition yourself. Use the price checker to compare relevant asking prices. Choose a selling strategy, save a draft and finish publishing on your chosen marketplace.

When to research further

A modern reissue can reproduce an old logo. If an item's value depends on age, provenance or rarity, compare the exact label to a documented reference and obtain specialist advice when appropriate. See our vintage identification guide.

VintFlow AI offers editable suggestions, not certified valuations or guaranteed sales. Always review your item and publish it yourself.
