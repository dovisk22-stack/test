How to write a Vinted listing with AI

A useful listing answers the buyer’s main questions: what the item is, who made it, what size it is and what condition it is in.

Start with a free account

Take photos that help the listing

Photograph one item in natural or even light. Include a full front view, back, brand label, size label and any wear or flaws. Avoid photos containing another person’s private information. VintFlow accepts up to six photos of the same item.

Generate a draft, then check it

Sign in with a verified account, open Sell, add the photos and select Analyse photos. VintFlow suggests a title, brand, size and description from the visible details. Check the original label, add the true condition and correct anything uncertain. The AI cannot authenticate a brand or reliably assess wear from every angle.

Example listing

Example only: “Nike grey pullover hoodie – size M” is easier to search than “Nice hoodie.” A description might say: “Grey pullover hoodie with a front pocket and drawstring hood. Label shows size M. Light wear on the cuffs; see close-up photos.” Only include those details if they are true of your item.

Publish on Vinted yourself

Review your asking price, copy the title and description, then open Vinted and upload the original photos. VintFlow saves your draft locally but does not publish or operate your Vinted account.

VintFlow is independent and not affiliated with Vinted. AI suggestions and price estimates require review. You publish your own listings. Free scans require a verified account and service availability.
