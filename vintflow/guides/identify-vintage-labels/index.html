How to identify vintage clothing labels

Read and compare brand labels, care tags and manufacturing clues to investigate the age of vintage garments.

Capture the label clearly

Take a close, sharp photo with enough light to reproduce small lettering. Include the full label and the reverse if it has additional text. Photograph attached care tags, country of manufacture, size and model codes separately.

Compare typography and layout

A brand can change logos, font, label colour and material over time, and can also reproduce old designs. Compare several dated examples from the Vintage Fashion Guild Label Resource or reliable brand archives rather than assuming a matching font proves an era.

Use care tags as supporting evidence

Language, symbols, company identification and manufacturing information may help narrow the possibilities. Requirements differ between countries and periods. Check the reference applicable to that garment. A country of manufacture or one symbol alone is rarely enough to establish a year.

Document uncertainty

Keep a photo of the label and record the reference you compared it to. If text is unclear, do not invent missing letters or a brand. VintFlow's photo identifier can help suggest a search term, but attribution and dating still need review.
