Help & support

How accurate are prices?

Market prices come from indexed listings, not confirmed sales. Review the exact condition and source links. The separate asking-price guide is an estimate.

Will VintFlow publish my listing?

No. Review your draft, copy the details and upload your original photos on Vinted yourself.

Where is my inventory saved?

Inventory is stored on this browser, not synced between devices. Export a backup under Settings before clearing browser data.

How your data is used

Account details and AI usage are stored with Supabase; inventory is stored in this browser. Cloudflare processes photos you choose to scan. Tavily may receive detected item descriptors for marketplace comparisons; your photos are not sent to Tavily. Stripe processes payments and billing information; VintFlow stores a customer reference to verify your plan. Free usage guards retain keyed email and browser hashes briefly. To prevent repeated introductory offers, VintFlow retains a keyed hash of the verified email and the account reference while the one-trial offer is available, including after account deletion. No card number is stored by VintFlow. Contact the support email for privacy requests. You can delete your account in Profile after outstanding billing has been resolved; legally required billing records may remain.
