Write a description buyers can use

An effective description tells a buyer what the item is, what size appears on the label, its colour and condition, and any relevant measurements or defects. Avoid keyword stuffing and don't claim a garment is authentic, vintage or new unless you can support it.

A practical example

ILLUSTRATIVE DRAFT · NOT A REAL LISTINGNavy crew-neck sweatshirt – size M

Classic navy sweatshirt with ribbed cuffs and hem. Label size M. Please check the measurements before purchasing. Add the actual condition and disclose any wear.

AI drafts are starting points. Replace any example wording with facts about your own garment.

Generate and review

Scan your item in VintFlow, review the proposed title and description, select the true condition and add details such as measurements. Select an asking price, copy the edited listing, or save it in Inventory. Publish yourself on Vinted using original photos.

What should I not include?

Do not describe flaws as absent without checking, take another seller's images or assume a label size matches modern fit. Read Vinted's uploading guidance and check our listing writing guide.

VintFlow AI offers editable suggestions, not certified valuations or guaranteed sales. Always review your item and publish it yourself.
