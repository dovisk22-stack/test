How much is my vintage clothing worth?

Work through a valuation worksheet and compare like-for-like examples before pricing your vintage garments.

Build a small comparison sheet

For each relevant example, note the marketplace, exact garment, brand, model or period, labelled size, condition, price, price type (asking or sold), and date. Aim for several genuinely comparable items instead of filling a list with unrelated garments.

Separate the types of evidence

A £60 listing that is still available does not establish that buyers will pay £60. Sold data, where verifiable, is stronger evidence of a transaction. EBay offers Product Research to sellers. Consider shipping separately and be cautious about accepted offers when the actual amount is unknown.

Use the middle of the credible sample

Example: five comparable asking prices are £12, £14, £16, £18 and £45. The median is £16. Investigate whether the £45 listing is a rarer model before using it as an anchor. This illustrative example is not evidence of any specific garment's market value.

Adjust only for known differences

Compare your garment's wear and repairs with the sample. Condition, size and exact model can change demand. For an exceptional or potentially valuable piece, consult a specialist rather than relying on a broad estimate. Use the value checker to gather starting points.

VintFlow AI offers editable suggestions, not certified valuations or guaranteed sales. Always review your item and publish it yourself.
