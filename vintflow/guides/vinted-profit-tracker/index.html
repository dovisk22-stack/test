How to track your Vinted sales and profit

Revenue is not the same as profit. Keeping a simple inventory helps you understand what your secondhand selling actually earns.

Start with a free account

Keep the cost of every item

Record the individual purchase cost, asking price, title and condition. If you bought several items together, allocate the bundle cost consistently so the total recorded matches what you paid.

Calculate the profit after a sale

A simple calculation is actual sale proceeds minus purchase cost minus fees and postage you personally paid. Example: if a top sells for £12, cost £4 and you paid £1 in expenses, the recorded profit is £7. Keep buyer-paid charges separate.

Use inventory statuses

In VintFlow, save a draft, mark it listed after you publish on Vinted, then mark it sold when a sale is completed. Enter the actual sale price and seller-paid expenses. The dashboard summarises the sales you have recorded; it does not fetch sales from Vinted.

Keep a backup

Export your inventory from Settings before clearing browser data, moving to a different device or changing domains. Inventory is stored in your browser, not synced in the cloud. Account and membership information are separate.
