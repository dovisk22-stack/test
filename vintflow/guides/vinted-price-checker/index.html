How to estimate clothing resale prices on Vinted

There is no single correct resale price. Brand, exact model, size, condition and demand all affect what a buyer may pay.

Try free pricing tool

Compare like for like

Look for the same item type and brand, then narrow by size, model, colour and condition. A new-with-tags jacket is not the same comparison as a worn one. Avoid using one expensive listing as your only reference.

Understand asking prices versus actual sales

A seller’s asking price is not proof that an item sold. On eBay, check completed and sold listings where available, and distinguish postage and fees from the item price. Indexed prices may be old or the item may no longer be available.

Use VintFlow as a starting point

After a photo scan, VintFlow can search for relevant indexed listings on Vinted, eBay and Depop. When there are enough readable comparison prices, it may display a comparison-based estimate and a range of selected prices. If evidence is limited, it may show approximate guidance or no estimate. Quick Sale, Recommended and Higher Return options are proposed asking prices, not guaranteed outcomes.

Choose your own price

Check the links and the marketplace’s pricing guidance, choose the actual condition and select a price that suits your priorities. Price lower if a quick sale matters, or try a higher ask if you can wait. No tool can guarantee a sale.
