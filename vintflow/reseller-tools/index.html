A straightforward selling routine

Spend less time retyping details by storing reviewed drafts and recording stock in one workspace. Open the scanner when adding a garment, check the price and save the draft. After you publish manually, mark it listed and record the actual selling price and expenses when it sells.

Keep the business under your control

VintFlow is not an auto-posting extension or a marketplace account integration. You copy and publish listings yourself. Check the marketplace's selling rules, including commercial selling requirements if sourcing stock for profit. Inventory is stored in your browser workspace; export backups when needed.

VintFlow AI offers editable suggestions, not certified valuations or guaranteed sales. Always review your item and publish it yourself.
